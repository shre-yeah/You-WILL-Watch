Hey there!
This is YouWILLWatch. A Chrome extension to help you complete your YouTube lectures by forcing you to sit down till you finish the video.
Here's how this works.
There are two modes as per your required needs.
1) Safe Mode:
  We enforce accountability by asking you what you paused for each time you pause your YouTube video. And you get two options-
  a) Break, in which case we generously grant you a 5 minute break to recover and rest before auto resuming your video.
  And option b) procrastion- in which we just start the video without any consideration. Lol.
  Further the video will auto resume eitherways in 1 minute. The extension gives you a minute relief in case you're taking notes or something
3) Strict Mode:
  a) This is for your actual strict YouTube lectures.
  b) You set a timer for how many ever minutes you wanna focus for
  c) Now your video would autoresume in 1 minute no matter what. So be prepared and be focused!
  d) And I am an annoying person concerned about your goals. And to make sure you're focusing, we have notification reminders if you switch your tabs or start doing something else on the same tab.

Its a very annoying extension, I understand. So you can enable and disable the extension as per your needs. But hey anything for accountability and actually being able to finish your videos right?

Cheers!

Installation Guide! Because this isn't on Google Webstore yet.
1) Simply clone this repository wherever you like
2) Go to chrome://extensions (or go to Manage Extensions)
3) Toggle Developer mode on
4) Click Load Unpacked and select the folder where you cloned the repo in
5) The extension will be ready to use
6) Oh you can also just download the zip folder directly. LOL.
