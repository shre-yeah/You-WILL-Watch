Here is a playful but annoying extension to help you stay focused and finally finish those YouTube lectures!

Here is what it does

  • Auto resumes your video in a minute when you pause it. A minute delay to jot down something if you must.
  • Let's you switch between strict and safe modes.
  • Safe mode allows for some flexibilty but strict mode keeps you on track.
  • Will annoyingly nag you when you switch tabs in strict mode. It's annoying, trust me. Don't switch tabs.
  • Oh and your YouTube suggestions sidebar is gonna get hidden. Ehehe.
  • Tracks simple stats to help build focus habits.
You might have to give some permissions for the extension to work properly however.
  • Permission to read and change data on youtube.com to control the video playback.
  • Permission to access browser tabs to detect when you switch away from the YouTube tab in strict mode.
  • Permission to send notificatins to alert when you switch tabs in a focus session.

Quick start

  1. Open a YouTube video tab.
  2. Click the extension icon and configure the mode.
  3. Adjust timings for strict mode if you select it, or go with safe mode.
  4. Try staying on the tab — the extension will help you return.
